What to check before paying more for any rug

  • Inspect the reverse: woven structure or secondary glued backing?
  • Ask how the fringe and outer edges are constructed.
  • Confirm exact fiber composition and cleaning instructions.
  • Request product-specific adhesive / emissions documentation if relevant.
  • Evaluate durability by fiber, density, traffic, maintenance and climate.
